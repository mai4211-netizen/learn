## 一、Part 2 修改与验收原则

1. **验收优先级：切题与内容合理 → 口语自然、表达流畅 → 减少记忆负担 → 模板与形式统一。** 后一项不能损害前一项。
2. **先沿题目回答。** 覆盖题卡要点，人物、地点、事件、感受和理由之间有清晰逻辑；不需要严格一条提示对应一段，也不为了凑模块而重复。
3. **目标口语 6–6.5 分。** 使用可以自然说出来的短句和熟悉词汇，避免复杂从句、书面化表达及难记的新信息；有足够细节，不把答案压成提纲。
4. **约 180 词只是参考。** 根据题目和内容调整篇幅，优先确保能连贯说约 2 分钟；不为达字数硬加故事，也不为缩短而丢掉切题信息。
5. **尽量复用已熟悉的故事、人物、句型和逻辑链。** 尤其复用问题→尝试→结果、原看法→经历→变化、特点→具体行为→评价、信息来源→内容→反应等自然结构；不要求所有题目生搬硬套四套框架。
6. **同一故事可以回答不同题，但必须改变答题重心。** 例如 Q16 与 Q47 复用广州森林公园骑行与湖景，Q16 强调为什么喜欢，Q47 强调为什么推荐；不能为复用率硬改答题对象。
7. **合理虚构可以接受。** Part 2 练习不要求所有细节真实；要求题内自洽、具体而可信。不因某段经历并非现实发生就必须换故事，也不强求跨题人物背景完全一致。
8. **减少无效重复，不减少有用信息。** 同一篇里一句话如果没有提供新细节、原因或结果，就删或替换；必要的主题呼应可保留。避免强行引入新人物、新经历与冲突。
9. **修改以已确认版本为基准，最小必要改动。** 优先修切题问题、明显矛盾、过渡不自然和冗余；不要把单题反馈变成全题库规则，不擅自重排故事、删除必要内容或改回已否定的旧版本。
10. **加粗是记忆提示，不是随机强调。** 「我的加粗／推荐加粗／只看加粗」都应保留能够带出整段叙事的关键词或短语，不能只加粗孤立动词。个人在浏览器里保存的加粗范围不属于英文原稿。
11. **中文与音频必须跟随最终英文。** 中文应准确反映最终英文，最好按段对应；正文定稿后再批量更新 Jenny MP3，并核对题号、文本版本和缓存，避免音文不同步。
12. **Q47 已确定为广州郊外湖边森林公园。** 不再用奈良山顶覆盖。Q52 的喜欢理由是 K11 与品牌、艺术家合作的展览持续变化，而不是简单强调上面有办公室。

## 二、文稿范围与使用说明

- 共 **58 题**，Q01–Q58 全部收录；保留题号、题目、题卡要点、中文记忆链与最终英文答案。
- 英文答案直接提取自当前网站的最终覆盖文件；**不重新润色，不重新编故事**。每段之间保留空行，方便朗读与后续修订。
- 中文资料分两种：**「中文逐段对应」**是最终覆盖文件中已同步的译文；**「中文概述（原站参考）」**是站内较早的概述，不视为最终英文的逐句译文。这种区分可以避免把旧中文内容误当作已校准版本。
- 网站的「我的加粗」属于本机浏览器存储，无法从公开仓库导出；本 Markdown 不擅自重建个人标注。Jenny MP3 属于独立音频资源，不包含在文本文件中。

### Q01 · 喜欢的公共建筑

**题目：** Describe a building you enjoy visiting (e.g. a library or shopping mall)

**题卡要点：** Location of this building；How you first learned about it；Its appearance；Why you enjoy visiting it

**中文记忆链：** K11市中心 → 朋友推荐 → 现代建筑/艺术展 → 咖啡与设计灵感

**最终英文答案 · 153 words**

I'd like to talk about K11, a building in downtown Guangzhou that I enjoy visiting. It has several floors of shops, with offices above them.

I first heard about K11 from a friend who likes art and design. She told me that its public spaces were more creative than those in a normal shopping centre, so we decided to visit together.

From the outside, it looks like a modern commercial building. Inside, there are artworks, colourful lights and small design displays. The spaces feel more interesting than those in an ordinary mall.

We sometimes go there for coffee and take photos of interesting details. I do not always buy anything there. Sometimes I just walk around and look at the design.

I enjoy K11 because it brings art into an everyday place. It is also easy to reach by public transport, so I can meet a friend there without making a special trip.

**中文逐段对应（网站最终稿）**

**1.** 我想介绍广州中心城区的 K11，一栋我喜欢去逛的建筑。它有好几层商店，上面还有办公空间。

**2.** 我最早是从一位喜欢艺术和设计的朋友那里听说 K11 的。她说这里的公共空间比普通购物中心更有创意，所以我们决定一起去看看。

**3.** 从外面看，它像一栋现代商业建筑。里面有艺术品、彩色灯光和小型设计陈列，整体空间比普通商场更有意思。

**4.** 我们有时会去那里喝咖啡、拍一些有意思的细节。我并不总是买东西，有时只是四处走走，看看设计。

**5.** 我喜欢 K11，因为它把艺术带进了日常生活。这里乘公共交通也很方便，所以我可以轻松约朋友见面，不需要特意安排一趟行程。

### Q02 · 不喜欢的短途旅行

**题目：** Describe a short trip you often take but do not like

**题卡要点：** Where you travel to；When you usually make this trip；Your reason for making it；Why you dislike this regular trip

**中文记忆链：** 大夫山 → 周末午后骑车 → 天气太热 → 更想早上去

**最终英文答案 · 179 words**

I'd like to talk about a short trip to Dafu Mountain Forest Park in Guangzhou that I often take but do not really enjoy.

My friend enjoys cycling there, so she often asks me to go with her at the weekend. We usually make the trip after lunch because that is when we both have free time.

When we arrive, we rent bikes near the entrance and ride along the paths beside the lake. The trees and water look beautiful, and we can chat while riding. However, the park is quite large, so we have to cycle for a while before we reach some of the quieter areas.

The main problem is the afternoon heat. Guangzhou can be extremely hot, and after a while I feel tired and sweaty. I just want to find some shade and rest. Sometimes I think staying at home with the air conditioner would be much easier.

I still go because I enjoy spending time with my friend. But the strong sun makes the trip exhausting, so I would prefer going in the morning.

**中文逐段对应（网站最终稿）**

**1.** 我想说一次不太喜欢的短途旅行，是经常去广州大夫山森林公园。

**2.** 朋友喜欢在那里骑车，所以周末常约我一起去。我们通常午饭后出发，因为那时两个人都有空。

**3.** 到达后，我们会在入口附近租自行车，沿着湖边小路骑行。树木和湖水很漂亮，骑车时也能聊天。不过公园很大，要骑一段路才能到比较安静的地方。

**4.** 最大的问题是下午太热。广州的午后气温很高，骑一会儿我就觉得又累又出汗，只想找阴凉处休息。有时我甚至觉得待在家里吹空调更轻松。

**5.** 我还是会陪朋友去，但强烈的阳光让这趟旅行很累，所以我更愿意早上去。

### Q03 · 拥挤地方

**题目：** Describe a crowded place that you have visited

**题卡要点：** Where it was；When you visited；The person who went with you；What you did there；How you felt about that visit

**中文记忆链：** K11周末下午 → 和朋友去 → 楼层/咖啡区拥挤 → 体验很累

**最终英文答案 · 163 words**

I'd like to talk about K11, a shopping centre in downtown Guangzhou that I visited with a friend one weekend afternoon.

The building has several floors of shops, offices and art displays. That day, the escalators and food area were packed. We had to walk slowly, and it was difficult to find a seat.

We moved from floor to floor, but many shops were chain stores we had seen before. Some areas were also closed for renovation, so there was less to look at than we expected.

We stayed for nearly two hours but bought almost nothing. In the end, we just got coffee and left. Even the café area was full, so we had to wait before ordering. The noise also made it difficult to have a proper conversation.

I do not mind a busy place if there is something special to see. However, that visit felt stressful because the crowds made everything slower. I prefer going to K11 on weekday mornings.

**中文逐段对应（网站最终稿）**

**1.** 我想介绍广州中心的 K11，一家购物中心。有个周末下午，我和一位朋友一起去了那里。

**2.** 这栋楼有几层，里面有商店、办公室和艺术展品。那天扶梯和餐饮区都挤满了人，我们只能慢慢走，也很难找到座位。

**3.** 我们一层层逛，但很多店都是以前见过的连锁店。有些区域也在装修，所以能逛的东西比预期少。

**4.** 我们在那里待了将近两个小时，却几乎什么都没买。最后只买了咖啡就离开。连咖啡区也坐满了人，点单前还得等一会儿。噪声也让我们很难好好聊天。

**5.** 如果有特别的东西可看，我并不介意人多。但那次让我觉得很有压力，因为人群让一切都变慢了。我更喜欢工作日早上去 K11。

### Q04 · 学习并喜欢历史的人

**题目：** Describe a person who learns and loves history

**题卡要点：** Who the person is；How they study history；What makes them interested in history；Your opinion of this person

**中文记忆链：** 陶艺朋友 → 研究老陶器 → 借用传统细节做新设计 → 我欣赏她

**最终英文答案 · 169 words**

The person I'd like to talk about is a friend who enjoys learning about Chinese pottery history. She makes pottery and runs a small studio. She is curious about how traditional bowls, shapes and patterns have changed over time.

One example is the way she develops new pieces. She often studies old pottery in museums or online, then uses one small detail as inspiration for a modern design. She makes colourful cups, small sculptures and character-shaped pieces. She does not simply copy the original objects.

She usually shapes the clay by hand and fires it in a kiln. When she has time, she compares pieces from different periods and explains what makes them different. I have watched her make small changes to a design, and she can do it very quickly.

I like her because she connects history with something people can see and use today. She makes the subject feel practical rather than distant. So I think she is a good example of someone who genuinely enjoys history.

**中文概述（原站参考，非逐句翻译）**

我想说一位喜欢研究历史的朋友，尤其是中国陶艺史。她是一名陶艺工作者，经营着小工作室，常在博物馆或网上研究古老的碗、器型和图案。她不直接照搬旧设计，而是从中汲取灵感，制作彩色杯子、小雕塑和角色造型的作品，同时坚持手工塑形、用窑烧制。我看过她在工作室操作，能很快调整作品的细节。我喜欢她保留传统技术、又让成品看起来现代的方式。她把历史真正运用在作品里，让那些旧知识变成看得见、做得出的东西。

### Q05 · 小时候学到的新技能

**题目：** Describe a new skill you learned when you were a child

**题卡要点：** The skill you learned；Who taught you；Your learning process；How learning it made you feel

**中文记忆链：** 冰皮月饼 → 妈妈教 → 包馅/压模 → 失败再尝试 → 做成功

**最终英文答案 · 174 words**

I'd like to talk about a skill I learned as a child: making snow-skin mooncakes.

One Mid-Autumn Festival when I was little, my mother taught me to make them at home. She wanted us to make something together instead of buying everything from a shop.

First, she showed me how to wrap sweet filling inside soft dough. Then we pressed the dough into a mould to make a pattern on top. It looked very easy when she did it, so I thought I could do it quickly too.

The difficult part was controlling the pressure. I pressed mine too hard, and it came out in a strange shape. My mother laughed and showed me how to press more gently. The dough was also a little sticky, so I needed a few tries to get used to it.

In the end, I made one that looked right, and we ate the mooncakes together that evening. I felt proud because I had finished it myself. I still remember the steps and how patient my mother was.

**中文概述（原站参考，非逐句翻译）**

我小时候学会的一项技能是做冰皮月饼。有一年中秋节，家人在家里团聚，妈妈教年纪还小的我制作月饼。我们准备甜馅，把馅包进柔软的饼皮，再用模具压出花纹。我第一次用力过猛，月饼形状不太均匀，妈妈便教我轻一点。后来大家比较成品，笑那些奇形怪状的月饼，晚饭后配茶一起吃。自制月饼并不完美，但一起动手的过程最难忘。直到今天我还记得基本步骤，因为这是和家人一起学的，而不是从食谱或课堂上学来的。

### Q06 · 尊敬的比你年长的人

**题目：** Describe someone older than you that you admire

**题卡要点：** Who they are；How you came to know them；Things you like doing together；How you feel about them

**中文记忆链：** 陶艺工作坊认识 → 一起做陶 → 她耐心指导 → 尊敬她

**最终英文答案 · 186 words**

The person I'd like to talk about is a friend who runs a small pottery studio. I first met her at a pottery workshop, and we have stayed in touch since then. She is a few years older than me, and I have always enjoyed seeing the things she makes.

One day, she invited me to her studio and taught me to make a small animal from clay. I wanted to make a rabbit, but the shape slowly turned into something closer to a bear. She showed me how to keep my hands steady and control the pressure.

She did not take the clay away and fix it for me. Instead, she explained what I could change and let me try again. The final piece was not perfect, but I was proud that I had finished it myself.

We still visit her studio, talk about her work and share photos of new pieces. I enjoy spending time with her because she never makes me feel silly when I am learning something new.

I respect her because she is patient and lets people learn from their mistakes.

**中文逐段对应（网站最终稿）**

**1.** 我想介绍一位朋友，她经营着一间小陶艺工作室。我是在一次陶艺工作坊认识她的，从那以后我们一直保持联系。她比我大几岁，我一直很喜欢看她制作的作品。

**2.** 有一天，她邀请我去工作室，教我用黏土做一只小动物。我本来想做兔子，最后却慢慢变得更像一只熊。她教我怎样稳住双手、控制力度。

**3.** 她没有拿走黏土替我修好，而是告诉我可以调整哪些地方，让我自己再试一次。最后的作品并不完美，但我很自豪，因为这是我自己完成的。

**4.** 我们现在仍会去她的工作室，聊聊她的作品，也会分享新作品的照片。我喜欢和她相处，因为在学新东西时，她从不会让我觉得自己很笨。

**5.** 我尊敬她，因为她很有耐心，也愿意让别人从错误中学习。

### Q07 · 嘈杂地

**题目：** Describe a noisy place you have been to

**题卡要点：** Where the place is；When you visited it；What you did there；Why you consider it noisy

**中文记忆链：** 香港演唱会 → 去年和两位朋友 → 全场合唱 → 很吵但兴奋

**最终英文答案 · 168 words**

I'd like to talk about a very noisy K-pop concert I attended in Hong Kong last year.

My two close friends and I had been fans of the same group for years, so we were excited long before the show started. We had dinner near the venue and talked about the songs we hoped to hear.

When the lights went down, the crowd started cheering and singing along. I could hardly hear my friends beside me. Even during the quieter songs, people were clapping and shouting the members' names. There were huge screens and strong lights, and the sound seemed to come from every direction.

The loudest moment was when the singers asked everyone to sing along. The whole venue became even louder, and my friends and I shouted the lyrics together. I could feel the music through the floor.

Normally I would find that volume tiring, but that night I enjoyed it because everyone was cheering for the same performance. It made the concert exciting and unforgettable.

**中文概述（原站参考，非逐句翻译）**

我记得最吵的地方是香港的一场 K-pop 演唱会。我和两位好友一起去，我们喜欢这个组合很多年，所以演出前就很兴奋。开场前我们在场馆附近吃饭，还讨论希望听到哪些歌。灯光一暗，观众开始欢呼和合唱，我几乎听不到身旁朋友说话。最后组合唱了粉丝歌，还说“明年见”。散场后虽然很累，我们仍买了饮料，一路聊到酒店。平时这样的噪声会让我烦躁，但在演唱会上大家都为同一场表演激动，反而让我很享受，尽管耳朵也累坏了。

### Q08 · 想颁布的环保法律

**题目：** Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce

**题卡要点：** What kind of law you would propose；Why people should follow it；Whether people would welcome the law；Your feelings about this proposal

**中文记忆链：** 德国退瓶制度 → 积分奖励 → 减少塑料 → 方便/公众支持

**最终英文答案 · 157 words**

I'd like to talk about a law that would reward people for returning plastic bottles for recycling.

I got the idea after reading about a bottle-return system in Germany. People can take empty plastic drink bottles to a recycling machine in a supermarket. They get back the deposit they paid when they bought the drinks.

In my version, people could receive a few points or a small shopping discount for every bottle they return. They could still buy bottled drinks as usual, so the law would not take away their choices.

I think people should follow it because plastic waste is a serious problem, and returning bottles is a simple way to reduce it. The machines are easy to use, and shops could put clear signs near the entrance.

I would feel happy if this law were introduced because it would make recycling part of an ordinary shopping trip, rather than something people have to plan separately.

**中文逐段对应（网站最终稿）**

**1.** 我想介绍一项鼓励人们归还塑料瓶进行回收的环保法律。这个想法来自德国的瓶子回收押金制度。

**2.** 在德国，人们可以把空塑料饮料瓶带到超市的回收机器。购买饮料时支付的押金，会在归还空瓶后退回来。

**3.** 如果由我来设计，我会给每个归还的瓶子一些积分或小额购物折扣。人们仍然可以照常购买瓶装饮料，不会失去选择的自由。

**4.** 我认为大家应该遵守这项规定，因为塑料垃圾是个严重问题，归还瓶子是减少垃圾的简单方式。机器也容易使用，商店可以在入口附近放清楚的指示。

**5.** 如果这项法律能实施，我会很开心，因为它能让回收成为日常购物的一部分，而不是一件需要专门安排的事。

### Q09 · 遇到的科技问题

**题目：** Describe a challenging technological problem you faced

**题卡要点：** The technical problem；When and where it happened；What made it challenging；How you managed to solve it

**中文记忆链：** 手表电池掉电 → 在家发现 → 更新系统 → 电池恢复

**最终英文答案 · 161 words**

I'd like to talk about a problem I had with my smartwatch. Its battery suddenly started running out very quickly.

I bought it about a year ago to track my exercise and sleep. One evening at home, I noticed that the battery hardly lasted until the end of the day. I usually check my steps after work, so I use it almost every day.

At first, I thought the battery might be getting old. I tried charging it fully and turning off some functions, but it still lost power too quickly. I was worried that I might need to pay for a new battery, so I wanted to find a simple solution.

Then I searched online and found people with the same problem. One comment suggested updating the system. I followed the steps and installed the update.
