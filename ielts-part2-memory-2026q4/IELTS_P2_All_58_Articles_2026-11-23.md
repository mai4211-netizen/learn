## Q01｜喜欢的公共建筑
**题目：** Describe a building you enjoy visiting (e.g. a library or shopping mall)

### English answer

I'd like to talk about K11, a building in downtown Guangzhou that I enjoy visiting. It has several floors of shops, with offices above them.

I first heard about K11 from a friend who likes art and design. She told me that its public spaces were more creative than those in a normal shopping centre, so we decided to visit together.

From the outside, it looks like a modern commercial building. Inside, there are artworks, colourful lights and small design displays. The spaces feel more interesting than those in an ordinary mall.

We sometimes go there for coffee and take photos of interesting details. I do not always buy anything there. Sometimes I just walk around and look at the design.

I enjoy K11 because it brings art into an everyday place. It is also easy to reach by public transport, so I can meet a friend there without making a special trip.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍广州中心城区的 K11，一栋我喜欢去逛的建筑。它有好几层商店，上面还有办公空间。

我最早是从一位喜欢艺术和设计的朋友那里听说 K11 的。她说这里的公共空间比普通购物中心更有创意，所以我们决定一起去看看。

从外面看，它像一栋现代商业建筑。里面有艺术品、彩色灯光和小型设计陈列，整体空间比普通商场更有意思。

我们有时会去那里喝咖啡、拍一些有意思的细节。我并不总是买东西，有时只是四处走走，看看设计。

我喜欢 K11，因为它把艺术带进了日常生活。这里乘公共交通也很方便，所以我可以轻松约朋友见面，不需要特意安排一趟行程。

## Q02｜不喜欢的短途旅行
**题目：** Describe a short trip you often take but do not like

### English answer

I'd like to talk about a short trip to Dafu Mountain Forest Park in Guangzhou that I often make with a friend. I don't like it when we have to go in the afternoon.

My friend enjoys cycling there, so she often asks me to go at weekends. We prefer going in the morning because it's cooler. But when we're only free after lunch, I don't enjoy the trip as much.

We rent bikes near the entrance and ride along the paths beside the lake. The trees and water look nice, and we chat while riding. The paths near the entrance are often busy, so we cycle further into the park to get away from the crowds.

The main problem is the afternoon heat. Guangzhou can be extremely hot, and after a while I feel tired and sweaty. There are also bugs near the trees and water, which makes me uncomfortable. I just want to find some shade and rest.

I still want to spend time with my friend, but on those hot afternoons I'd rather meet her somewhere with air conditioning, like a café. That way we could chat without feeling exhausted by the sun.

### 中文翻译

我想讲一段经常和朋友去广州大夫山森林公园的短途行程。我不喜欢的是不得不在下午出发的时候。

朋友喜欢在那里骑车，所以周末经常约我一起去。我们更喜欢早上去，因为天气凉爽。但如果只有午饭后才有空，我就没有那么喜欢这趟行程。

我们会在入口附近租自行车，沿着湖边小路骑行。树木和湖水很好看，我们可以边骑车边聊天。入口附近的小路常常很拥挤，所以我们会往公园里面骑，避开人群。

最大的问题是下午太热。广州有时非常炎热，骑一会儿我就又累又出汗。树和湖水附近还有虫子，让我很不舒服。我只想找树荫休息。

我仍然想和朋友相处，但在这么热的下午，我宁愿和她去有空调的地方，比如咖啡馆。这样我们就能聊天，而不用被太阳晒得筋疲力尽。

## Q03｜拥挤地方
**题目：** Describe a crowded place that you have visited

### English answer

I'd like to talk about K11, a shopping centre in downtown Guangzhou that I visited with a friend one weekend afternoon.

The building has several floors of shops, offices and art displays. That day, the escalators and food area were packed. We had to walk slowly, and it was difficult to find a seat.

We walked from floor to floor, hoping to find something interesting. However, many shops were chain stores we had seen before, and some areas were closed for renovation.

We stayed for nearly two hours and bought almost nothing. Even the café area was crowded, so we had to wait before ordering. We finally had a coffee, then went home instead of walking around more shops. The noise also made it difficult to have a proper conversation.

I do not mind a busy place if there is something special to see. However, that visit felt stressful because the crowds made everything slower. I prefer going to K11 on weekday mornings.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍广州中心的 K11，一家购物中心。有个周末下午，我和一位朋友一起去了那里。

这栋楼有几层，里面有商店、办公室和艺术展品。那天扶梯和餐饮区都挤满了人，我们只能慢慢走，也很难找到座位。

我们从一层逛到另一层，希望找到有趣的东西。但许多店都是以前见过的连锁店，还有一些区域正在装修。

我们待了将近两个小时，却几乎什么都没买。连咖啡区也很拥挤，点单前还要等。最后我们喝了杯咖啡就回家了，没有继续逛其他商店。周围的噪声也让我们很难好好聊天。

如果有特别的东西可看，我并不介意人多。但那次让我觉得很有压力，因为人群让一切都变慢了。我更喜欢工作日早上去 K11。

## Q04｜学习并喜欢历史的人
**题目：** Describe a person who learns and loves history

### English answer

The person I'd like to talk about is a friend who enjoys learning about Chinese pottery history. She makes pottery and runs a small studio. She is curious about how traditional bowls, shapes and patterns have changed over time.

One example is the way she develops new pieces. She often studies old pottery in museums or online, then uses one small detail as inspiration for a modern design. She makes colourful cups, small sculptures and character-shaped pieces. She does not simply copy the original objects.

She usually shapes the clay by hand and fires it in a kiln. When she has time, she compares pieces from different periods and explains what makes them different. I have watched her make small changes to a design, and she can do it very quickly.

I like her because she connects history with something people can see and use today. She makes the subject feel practical rather than distant. So I think she is a good example of someone who genuinely enjoys history.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍一位喜欢研究中国陶艺历史的朋友。她是一名陶艺工作者，经营着小型工作室，很好奇传统陶碗的形状、器型和图案是怎样随着时间变化的。

比如，她在设计新作品时，经常先到博物馆或网上研究古老的陶器，再从中挑一个细节作为现代设计的灵感。她会做彩色杯子、小雕塑和角色造型的作品，而不是直接复制古董。

她通常用手给黏土塑形，再放进窑里烧制。有空时她也会比较不同时代的陶艺作品，解释它们有什么区别。我看过她调整作品细节，动作很快。

我喜欢她把历史和今天人们看得见、用得上的东西联系起来的方式。她让历史显得很实际，而不是遥不可及，所以我觉得她是真的热爱历史。

## Q05｜小时候学到的新技能
**题目：** Describe a new skill you learned when you were a child

### English answer

I'd like to talk about a skill I learned as a child: making snow-skin mooncakes.

One Mid-Autumn Festival when I was little, my mother taught me to make them at home. She thought it would be fun to make something together instead of buying everything from a shop.

First, she showed me how to put sweet filling inside the soft mooncake skin and close it. Then she pressed it into a mould to make a pattern on top. It looked easy when she did it, so I wanted to try.

I pressed the mould too hard, and my mooncake came out in a strange shape. My mother laughed and showed me how to press it more gently. The skin was a bit sticky, so I needed a few tries to get it right.

In the end, I made a mooncake that looked much better. We ate the mooncakes together that evening, and I felt really proud because I'd made one myself. I still remember the steps and how patient my mother was.

### 中文翻译

我想讲小时候学会的一项技能：制作冰皮月饼。

小时候有一年中秋节，妈妈在家教我做冰皮月饼。她觉得我们一起动手会很有趣，不必什么都从商店买。

她先教我把甜馅放进柔软的月饼皮里，再把它包好。然后用模具压出表面的花纹。妈妈做起来很轻松，我也想试试。

我把模具压得太用力，月饼形状变得很奇怪。妈妈笑了笑，教我轻一点压。月饼皮还有点黏，所以我试了几次才做好。

最后我做出了一个形状好得多的月饼。当天晚上我们一起吃月饼，我很自豪，因为这是自己亲手做的。我一直记得这些步骤，也记得妈妈很有耐心。

## Q06｜尊敬的比你年长的人
**题目：** Describe someone older than you that you admire

### English answer

The person I'd like to talk about is a friend who runs a small pottery studio. I first met her at a pottery workshop, and we have stayed in touch since then. She is a few years older than me, and I have always enjoyed seeing the things she makes.

One day, she invited me to her studio and taught me to make a small animal from clay. I wanted to make a rabbit, but the shape became too round, and I couldn't tell what animal it looked like. She suggested adding round ears and turning it into a bear.

She did not take the clay away and fix it for me. Instead, she explained what I could change and let me try again. The final piece was not perfect, but I was proud that I had finished it myself.

We still visit her studio, talk about her work and share photos of new pieces. I enjoy spending time with her because she never makes me feel silly when I am learning something new.

I respect her because she is patient and lets people learn from their mistakes.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍一位经营小型陶艺工作室的朋友。我最初在陶艺工作坊认识她，后来一直保持联系。她比我大几岁，我一直很喜欢看她的作品。

有一天她邀请我去工作室，教我用黏土做小动物。我本来想做兔子，但形状越来越圆，连自己都看不出像什么动物。她建议加上圆耳朵，把它变成熊。

她没有把黏土拿过去替我修好，而是告诉我可以调整哪里，让我自己再试一次。成品不是很完美，但我很自豪，因为是自己完成的。

我们现在还会去她的工作室，聊聊她的作品，分享新作品的照片。我喜欢和她相处，因为当我学习新东西时，她从来不会让我觉得自己很笨。

我尊敬她，因为她很有耐心，也愿意让别人从错误中学习。

## Q07｜嘈杂地
**题目：** Describe a noisy place you have been to

### English answer

I'd like to talk about a very noisy K-pop concert I attended in Hong Kong last year.

My two close friends and I had been fans of the same group for years, so we were excited long before the show started. We had dinner near the venue and talked about the songs we hoped to hear.

When the lights went down, the crowd started cheering. Once the music began, people started singing along. I could hardly hear my friends beside me. Even during the quieter songs, people clapped and shouted the members' names. There were huge screens and strong lights, and the sound seemed to come from every direction.

The loudest moment was when the singers asked everyone to sing along. The whole venue became even louder, and my friends and I shouted the lyrics together. I could feel the music through the floor.

Normally, that much noise would make me tired. But my friends and I were shouting much louder than we usually would, and the fans around us were doing the same. It felt good to let out some stress together. That is why I remember the noise as part of the fun, not something unpleasant.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍去年在香港参加的一场非常吵闹的 K-pop 演唱会。

我和两位好友喜欢同一个组合很多年了，所以演出还没开始就很兴奋。我们在场馆附近吃了晚饭，聊着希望听到的歌曲。

灯光暗下来时，观众开始欢呼。音乐响起后，大家才开始跟着唱，我几乎听不见身边的朋友说话。即使歌曲比较安静，大家也会鼓掌、喊成员的名字。那里有巨大的屏幕和强烈的灯光，声音像从四面八方传来。

最吵的时候是歌手邀请大家一起唱。整个场馆又变得更响，我和朋友也跟着全场一起大声唱歌词，甚至能透过地板感受到音乐的震动。

平时这么大的声音会让我觉得累。但那天我和朋友都喊出了平常不会有的音量，周围的歌迷也是如此。大家一起喊出来，感觉能暂时释放压力。所以我记住的是这种吵闹带来的快乐，而不是觉得它讨厌。

## Q08｜想颁布的环保法律
**题目：** Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce

### English answer

I'd like to talk about a law that would reward people for returning plastic bottles for recycling.

I first read about Germany's bottle-return system online. In Germany, people pay a small deposit when they buy certain bottled drinks. They can return the empty bottles to machines in supermarkets and get their money back.

In my version, people could receive a few points or a small shopping discount for every bottle they return. They could still buy bottled drinks as usual, so the law would not take away their choices.

I think people should follow it because plastic waste is a serious problem, and returning bottles is a simple way to reduce it. Supermarkets and shopping centres could put machines near their entrances, so people could return bottles during their normal shopping.

I would feel happy if this law were introduced because it would make recycling part of an ordinary shopping trip, rather than something people have to plan separately.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍一项鼓励人们归还塑料瓶进行回收的环保法律。这个想法来自德国的瓶子回收押金制度。

我最早在网上了解到德国的退瓶制度。在德国，人们购买某些瓶装饮料时会支付一笔小额押金，之后可以把空瓶送到超市的机器，拿回押金。

如果由我来设计，我会给每个归还的瓶子一些积分或小额购物折扣。人们仍然可以照常购买瓶装饮料，不会失去选择的自由。

我认为大家应该遵守这项规定，因为塑料垃圾是个严重问题，归还瓶子是减少垃圾的简单方式。超市和购物中心可以在入口附近设置机器，让人们平时购物时就能顺手退瓶。

如果这项法律能实施，我会很开心，因为它能让回收成为日常购物的一部分，而不是一件需要专门安排的事。

## Q09｜遇到的科技问题
**题目：** Describe a challenging technological problem you faced

### English answer

I'd like to talk about a problem I had with my smartwatch. Its battery suddenly started running out very quickly.

I bought it about a year ago to track my exercise and sleep. One evening at home, I noticed that the battery hardly lasted until the end of the day. I usually check my steps after work, so I use it almost every day.

At first, I thought the battery might be getting old. I tried charging it fully and turning off some functions, but it still lost power too quickly. I was worried the problem was serious enough that I might need a new watch, so I looked for a simple solution first.

Then I searched online and found people with the same problem. One comment suggested updating the system. I followed the steps and installed the update.

After the update, the battery lasted much longer and the watch worked normally again. I felt relieved because I didn't have to spend money on a new watch after all.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍一次智能手表出了问题的经历：它的电池突然耗电非常快。

我大约一年前买它来记录运动和睡眠。一天晚上在家，我发现电量几乎撑不到一天结束。我通常下班后会查看步数，所以几乎每天都用手表。

一开始我以为电池可能老化了。我试着把电充满、关闭部分功能，但电量还是掉得很快。我担心问题严重到可能需要买块新手表，所以先想找个简单的解决方法。

后来我上网搜索，发现其他人遇到过同样的问题。有条评论建议更新系统，我按照步骤安装了更新。

更新之后，电池续航恢复了正常，手表也能照常使用。我松了一口气，因为最后不用花钱买新手表了。

## Q10｜当地新闻
**题目：** Describe a piece of local news that people are interested in

### English answer

I'd like to talk about a piece of local news about a pottery market in Guangzhou.

I came across a short report about it on social media after work. I almost skipped it because I used to think pottery was old-fashioned. But some colourful pieces caught my eye, so I decided to watch it.

It showed local makers shaping clay and displaying their finished pieces. There were colourful cups, small sculptures and cute character-shaped objects, not just plain bowls.

A few young makers explained that they wanted more local people to visit the market and learn about traditional crafts. The event gave local artists a chance to show and sell their work. It also gave younger people something interesting to do at the weekend.

I think local people cared about this news because it supported local artists and brought something new to the community.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍一则关于广州陶艺市集的本地新闻。

下班后，我在社交媒体上看到一段关于它的短报道。我原本觉得陶艺很老派，差点划过去。但几个色彩鲜艳的作品吸引了我的注意，所以我决定继续看。

视频展示了本地创作者如何用手塑造黏土，并展示完成的作品。有彩色杯子、小雕塑和可爱的角色造型，而不只是普通的碗。

几位年轻创作者说，他们希望更多当地人来参观市集、了解传统手工艺。这个活动也让本地艺术家有机会展示和出售作品，并为年轻人的周末带来了有趣的活动。

这段报道吸引了我，因为陶艺作品看起来既现代又有趣。它改变了我对传统工艺的印象，也让我想亲自去市集看看。

我认为当地人关注这则新闻，是因为市集支持本地艺术家，也为社区带来新的活动。

## Q11｜长期追求/目标
**题目：** Describe a long-term goal or ambition you would like to achieve

### English answer

I'd like to talk about my long-term goal of building a small online art platform. I've wanted to work on an art project like this for a few years.

The idea became clearer after I saw how my friend Claire runs her online art business. Her platform works with independent artists and lets customers preview how a painting might look on their own wall before buying it.

For example, a customer can choose a painting and see how it might fit into a room. This solves a simple problem because people often find it difficult to imagine the size and colours of an artwork from a product photo.

For my own platform, I would first ask a few artists what they need and try the idea with a small group. I would also need to learn more about management, branding and communication. I would begin with a simple version and improve it after getting feedback.

I chose this goal because it combines my interests in art, design and technology. I hope it could also help independent artists reach more customers.

### 中文翻译

我想介绍自己的长期目标：建立一个小型线上艺术平台。我想做这样的艺术项目已经有好几年了。

看到朋友 Claire 经营自己的线上艺术生意后，这个想法变得更明确。她的平台与独立艺术家合作，让顾客在买画前预览作品挂在自己墙上的效果。

比如，顾客可以选一幅画，看看它放在房间里是否合适。这解决了一个简单的问题：只看商品照片时，人们往往难以想象作品实际的尺寸和颜色。

如果要做自己的平台，我会先问几位艺术家需要什么，再找一小群人尝试这个想法。我还需要进一步学习管理、品牌和沟通。我会从一个简单的版本开始，根据反馈逐渐改进。

我选择这个目标，因为它结合了我对艺术、设计和科技的兴趣。我也希望它能帮助独立艺术家接触更多顾客。

## Q12｜想从事医疗行业的人
**题目：** Describe someone who wants to work in the medical field

### English answer

The person I'd like to talk about is a former classmate of mine who wants to become a doctor. I met her at school more than ten years ago, and she has always been patient and organised.

She became interested in medicine after watching a Korean medical drama. The doctors had to stay calm, make quick decisions and help people in difficult situations. That made her respect the job and want to learn more about it.

Later, she did some volunteer work in her community. She helped with simple health activities, looked after people who needed help and organised small tasks with the other volunteers.

She thinks the experience taught her how to care for people and handle practical work. She has also read about the training doctors need and knows it takes many years. She understands that the work will not be easy, but she wants to use what she learns to help people.

I think she would suit this career because she is patient, organised and willing to keep studying.

### 中文翻译

她看了一部韩国医疗剧后开始对医学感兴趣。剧里的医生需要保持冷静、快速做决定，并在困难的情况下帮助病人，这让她很尊敬这份职业，也想进一步了解。

后来她参加了社区志愿活动，协助简单的健康项目，照顾有需要的人，并和其他志愿者一起安排一些小任务。

她觉得这些经历教会了自己如何关心别人、处理实际工作。她也查过成为医生需要接受的培训，知道这条路要花很多年。虽然工作不容易，但她希望用所学帮助别人。

我认为她适合这份职业，因为她有耐心、有条理，也愿意持续学习。

## Q13｜无聊的地方
**题目：** Describe a boring place

### English answer

I'd like to talk about K11 in Guangzhou, a place I found quite boring on one visit.

It is a large shopping centre in the city centre, with several floors of shops, offices and art displays. The building itself looks modern, but some parts can feel similar to an ordinary mall.

I went there with a friend on a weekend afternoon. We walked from floor to floor, hoping to find something interesting.
