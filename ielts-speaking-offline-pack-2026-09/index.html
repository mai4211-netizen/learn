先这样用：45 分钟一轮

不要从第一页顺着背。每一轮只做少量题，但必须开口。

时间 任务 动作

0-10 分钟 P1 热身 随机选 5 个 Topic，每题说 3-4 句。

10-30 分钟 P3 逻辑 选 3 道题，先用中文说清‘观点 -> 原因 -> 结果/例子’，再说英文。

30-40 分钟 对照答案 只找自己缺失的那一层，不抄难词。

40-45 分钟 复盘 记下 3 个可复用开头和 3 个容易卡住的话题。

P1 目标结构 直接回答 -> because -> 一个具体细节。控制在 20-30 秒。

P3 目标结构 观点 -> 原因 -> 影响 -> 例子/对比。控制在 40-55 秒。

01 Shoes

Which do you prefer, fashionable shoes or comfortable shoes?

中文逻辑｜舒适优先 -> 每天走路多 -> 好看的鞋也要能久穿
6-6.5 分

I prefer comfortable shoes because I walk quite a lot every day. Fashion matters to me, but I would not buy a pair if it hurt my feet. Ideally, I want shoes that look simple and feel comfortable for several hours.

7 分

参考 Comfort is definitely my first priority because I spend a lot of time on my feet. I still care about how shoes look, but I tend to choose simple designs that match most of my clothes. A stylish pair is not very useful to me if I can only wear it for half an hour.

02 Advertisement

Have you ever bought something because of an advertisement?

中文逻辑｜有 -> 手机广告展示功能 -> 看评价后购买 -> 广告吸引我但不是唯一原因
6-6.5 分

Yes, I once bought a pair of wireless earphones after seeing an online advertisement. The video showed the noise-cancelling feature clearly, so I became interested. I checked some reviews before ordering them, and they turned out to be quite useful.

7 分

参考 Yes, an online ad once introduced me to a pair of wireless earphones with active noise cancellation. The demonstration was convincing enough to catch my attention, although I still compared prices and read user reviews before placing the order. So the advert influenced me, but it was not the only reason I bought them.

01 城市、建筑与文化

Why do people visit historic buildings?

中文逻辑｜了解过去 -> 亲眼看到生活方式 -> 与文化身份建立联系
6-6.5 分

People visit historic buildings because they want to learn about past cultures and traditions. These places show how people used to live and work. Seeing them in person can make history easier to understand than simply reading a textbook.

7 分

参考 Many people are drawn to historic buildings because they offer a direct connection to earlier generations. Architecture, objects and even the layout of a building reveal how people once lived and what they valued. For visitors with family ties to the region, the experience can also strengthen their sense of cultural identity.

02 城市、建筑与文化

Is it important to protect historic buildings? Why?

中文逻辑｜文化记忆不可替代 -> 教育后代 -> 旅游带动本地经济
6-6.5 分

Yes, it is important because historic buildings show the culture and history of a city. They help younger people understand the past. They can also attract tourists and support local businesses.

7 分

参考 Protecting historic buildings is essential because they preserve a city's cultural memory in a physical form. Once they disappear, future generations lose a valuable way to understand how society developed. Well-maintained heritage sites can also attract visitors, which creates jobs and supports the local economy.

03 自然与环境

What can people gain from going to natural places?

中文逻辑｜安静环境 -> 降低压力 -> 鼓励运动 -> 更愿意保护自然
6-6.5 分

Natural places can help people feel less stressed because they are usually quiet and peaceful. They also encourage people to walk, cycle or hike. After spending time in a park or near a lake, many people feel calmer and more energetic.

7 分

参考 Natural environments offer both psychological and physical benefits. The quiet atmosphere gives people a break from constant noise and screens, while activities such as hiking or cycling improve fitness. Regular contact with nature can also make people more aware of environmental protection because they see what could be lost.

04 自然与环境

Which is more important, protecting the environment or developing the economy?

中文逻辑｜环境是经济基础 -> 污染难逆转 -> 发展应走可持续路线
6-6.5 分

I think environmental protection should come first because serious damage is difficult to repair. Clean air and water are also necessary for health, farming and tourism. Economic growth should continue, but it should not destroy the resources people depend on.

7 分

参考 Environmental protection deserves priority because a healthy economy ultimately depends on stable natural systems. Polluted water, damaged soil and extreme weather all create long-term costs for public health and industry. The practical goal should be sustainable growth, where investment and employment increase without exhausting the resources future generations will need.

Group learning gives people quick feedback and makes practice more enjoyable. You can ask questions and learn from other people's mistakes. Studying alone is quieter and more flexible, so it may be better when you need deep concentration.

7 分

参考 Learning in a group provides immediate feedback, shared motivation and opportunities to practise communication. Independent study, however, allows learners to control the pace and focus on their own weaknesses. I think group work suits discussion and practical tasks, while solo study is often better for reading or detailed revision.

06 学习与技能

Is it easier for children to learn new skills than for adults?

中文逻辑｜孩子适应快、不怕错、时间多 -> 成人目标清楚、自律强 -> 技能不同结论不同
6-6.5 分

In many cases, children learn faster because they are curious and not afraid of making mistakes. They also have fewer responsibilities, so they have more time to practise. Adults may learn more slowly, but they usually have clearer goals.

7 分

参考 Children often pick up languages, sports and creative skills quickly because they are highly adaptable and willing to experiment. Adults may need more repetition, yet they usually bring stronger self-discipline and a clearer purpose. The advantage therefore depends on the skill: children may learn pronunciation faster, while adults may handle complex planning better.

10 科技与教育

What are the advantages and disadvantages of AI?

6-6.5 分

AI can save time by summarising information and helping people with routine tasks. It can also support personalised learning. The disadvantages are that it sometimes gives wrong information and people may depend on it too much, so users still need to check the result.

7 分

参考 AI can improve productivity by handling repetitive work and giving learners support that matches their level. At the same time, its answers may contain errors or hidden bias, and excessive reliance can weaken independent judgement. The best approach is to treat AI as an assistant while keeping human review and responsibility.

参考 Advertising can provide useful information about new products and fund media that consumers access for free. 最后一页：可复用表达

把句子当成路标，不要连续堆砌。每个答案选 2-3 个就够。

直接观点

  • I think the main reason is that...
  • In most cases, I would say...
  • It largely depends on...

解释原因

  • This is mainly because...
  • One practical reason is that...
  • That tends to happen when...

结果与影响

  • This means that...
  • As a result, people are more likely to...
  • Over time, this can lead to...

举例与对比

  • For example,...
  • A simple example would be...
  • Compared with..., ... tends to...

让步

  • That said,...
  • There is a downside, though.
  • This is useful as long as...
