6–6.5 分

People visit historic buildings because they want to learn about past cultures and traditions. These places show how people used to live and work. Seeing them in person can make history easier to understand than simply reading a textbook.

7 分参考

Many people are drawn to historic buildings because they offer a direct connection to earlier generations. Architecture, objects and even the layout of a building reveal how people once lived and what they valued. For visitors with family ties to the region, the experience can also strengthen their sense of cultural identity.
